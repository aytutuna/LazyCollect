# IRCC sources

Checked 2026-11-23 on canada.ca and ircc.canada.ca. Help texts in the IRCC templates are plain-language summaries, not legal advice, and the checklist inside the applicant's own IRCC account always wins. Items marked unverified could not be read.

## Upload rules

Per-file size limit, from https://ircc.canada.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?qnum=1123&top=23 ("The file size limit varies depending on which online application account you use"):

| Account | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| IRCC secure account | 4 MB |
| IRCC Portal and the new Portal | 5 MB |
| Online citizenship application | 4 MB |
| Permanent Residence Portal (Express Entry) | 4 MB |
| Asylum portal | 4 MB |

Some forms and answers give less: 2 MB on https://ircc.canada.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?qnum=1447&top=14, and https://ircc.canada.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?qnum=1213 says a limit "can be as low as 2 MB". The export offers 2, 4, 5 and 10 MB, defaulting to 4. 10 MB was not found for any account.

Formats, from https://ircc.canada.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?qnum=1124: secure account .jpg .png .doc .tiff .pdf; Portal also .docx; Permanent Residence Portal needs .pdf for forms and documents (also .jpg .png .doc .docx). PDF and JPEG work everywhere.

File names, from qnum=1447: English or French letters, numbers, spaces, hyphens and the characters `/ & , . - : \ ( ) * # ; @`. Underscores are not on that list, so the export joins words with hyphens and drops accents and every other character. No maximum length was found; the export keeps names under 50 characters. IMM 0273 suggests "Last Name - First Name - Document type - #".

One file per slot: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/submit-requested-information-documents.html says to combine several documents or pages into one file. Several uploads to one slot keep only the last. Resolution: no minimum is stated; qnum=1213 says 96 or 150 DPI scans reduce size, which is why the export never goes below about 150 dpi at letter size. Password-protected PDFs, a maximum number of files and whether a translation shares a slot with its original are unverified.

Translations: certified English or French translation plus the original scan; an affidavit from the translator unless they are a Canadian certified translator; the applicant and close family cannot translate (study permit guide 5269, Express Entry apply page, citizenship pages).

## Checklists

- Study permit from outside Canada: guide 5269 https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides/guide-5269-applying-study-permit-outside-canada.html, documents https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/get-documents.html. Forms IMM 1294, IMM 5707, checklist IMM 5483. Attestation letter and exemptions: .../study-permit/get-documents/provincial-attestation-letter.html (valid until December 31, 2026 for this cap year). Funds: .../study-permit/get-documents/financial-support.html, C$23,448 for one person from September 1, 2026 (C$22,895 before). The documents page still shows an older table; the funds page was used. The Student Direct Stream status is unverified.
- PGWP: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/apply.html and .../eligibility.html. Apply within 180 days of completion; within 90 days of permit expiry to restore status. Language proof (level 7 university, 5 college) if the study permit application was on or after November 1, 2024; field of study for non-degree programs. Which supporting documents IRCC wants beyond the portal checklist is unverified.
- Work permit, outside Canada: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/permit-outside/forms-documents.html, guide 5487, form IMM 1295, optional checklist IMM 5488. Employer-specific: employer letter, reference letters, work proof, résumé; LMIA copy and number, or an offer of employment number (A and 7 digits); CAQ for paid Quebec work over 30 days; "Not applicable" page for unused employment slots.
- Work permit, inside Canada: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/permit-inside/forms-documents.html, guide 5553, form IMM 5710, optional checklist IMM 5556. Apply 30 days before expiry; restoration within 90 days. A spousal open work permit is separate (.../family-sponsorship/spouse-partner-children/spouse-common-law-partner-canada-open-work-permit.html) and not in a template; bridging open work permits are unverified.
- Visitor visa: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/apply-visitor-visa.html. Passport valid 6 months past travel; the page lists travel history (10 years), itinerary, bank statements, employer letter dated within 3 months, invitation and proof of relationship. IRCC's own kits disagree on 3 or 6 months of statements.
- Spousal or common-law sponsorship: guide 5289 https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides/guide-5289-sponsor-your-spouse-common-law-partner-conjugal-partner-dependent-child-complete-guide.html. Signed IMM 1344 and IMM 5532 are uploaded; IMM 0008, 5669 and 5406 are completed in the portal. Police certificates for countries lived in 6 months or more in a row after 18; medical exam for each person; common-law needs 12 months living together and IMM 5409; a registered marriage is needed, not a licence alone. The last part of the guide and the current document checklist number (IMM 5533 was seen) are unverified.
- Express Entry PR after the invitation: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/apply-permanent-residence.html and .../express-entry/documents/proof-funds.html. 60 days to apply; a personalized checklist. Proof of funds for one person was C$15,263 (page dated July 2025); exempt for Canadian Experience Class or a valid job offer with work authorization. The police certificate rule for Express Entry could not be read.
- Citizenship, adult: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-citizenship/adult-minor/how/adult-forms-documents.html, checklist CIT 0007 (https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/ircc/documents/pdf/english/kits/forms/cit0007/01-03-2024/cit0007e.pdf, may be replaced), form CIT 0002. Language proof between 18 and 54; police certificate for countries lived in 183 days or more in a row within 4 years; tax filing is checked with the CRA and the page does not ask for notices of assessment.
